01 / About

I’m an Embedded & Robotics Engineer focused on intelligent industrial automation.

I design and develop real-time control systems, embedded software, and intelligent automation solutions that connect hardware, software, sensors, and machines.My work spans from low-level embedded firmware and motor-control algorithms to industrial communication, robotics, automated testing, and AI-based systems.
